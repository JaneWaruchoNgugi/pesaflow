PESAFLOW — PITCH NOTES (KENYAN YOUTH)
=====================================

DON'T lead with "budgeting app" — that word makes people's eyes glaze.
LEAD with the emotional truth every young Kenyan lives:
  "Money comes in from the hustle... and disappears. You're working, but
   you have nothing to show for it, and you're one emergency away from
   Fuliza."
That's the wound. PesaFlow is the answer.

STRUCTURE THE WHOLE PITCH AS: PAIN -> MIRROR -> PATH
  1. Name the pain they feel (money vanishes, the Fuliza cycle).
  2. Show the mirror (the app reveals the truth with their own numbers).
  3. Offer the path (control -> savings -> wealth).
Youth pitches die when you lead with features; they land when you lead
with THEIR reality and make them feel seen.


THE THEMES THAT ACTUALLY LAND
-----------------------------

1. "BUILT FOR THE HUSTLE, NOT THE PAYSLIP" — your sharpest angle
   Most young Kenyans don't earn a fixed salary — they earn from boda,
   mitumba, freelance, content, small biz, casual gigs. Every foreign
   budgeting app assumes a monthly payslip and feels useless to them.
   PesaFlow already supports irregular/daily income streams — lean into
   this hard.
   Line: "The first money app that gets how you actually earn — daily,
   irregular, from three hustles at once." No competitor owns this message.

2. ESCAPE THE FULIZA / LOAN-APP TRAP (AND STAY OFF CRB)
   The deepest fear and daily reality. Fuliza, Tala, Branch, KCB-Mpesa —
   borrow to survive, repay, borrow again. Being listed on CRB blocks
   loans, some jobs, everything.
   Line: "See exactly where your money leaks, build a small emergency
   fund, and stop living on Fuliza — before CRB lists you." Control
   instead of borrowing.

3. THE MIRROR: WHERE YOUR MONEY REALLY GOES (YES, INCLUDING BETTING)
   Aviator, Betika, SportPesa quietly drain youth wallets, plus
   "soft life" / flex-culture spending. Don't preach — SHOW them the
   truth with their own numbers.
   Line: "Your M-Pesa doesn't lie. See what's really eating your money."
   People change when they see it, not when they're lectured.

4. FROM SURVIVING -> BUILDING (the aspiration flip)
   After the pain, sell the dream every youth has: a plot, a business,
   capital, freedom from black-tax pressure. Goals + emergency fund +
   net worth turn "broke till end month" into "I'm building something."
   Line (your own tagline — it's good): "Track your money. Grow your
   wealth. Sleep better."

5. THE MONEY COACH NOBODY GAVE THEM
   School never taught this. Frame the advisor/AI as a pocket financial
   coach that speaks their language (Swahili/Sheng), free, no shame,
   no bank manager judging them.


WHAT TO AVOID IN THE PITCH
--------------------------
- Don't say "financial discipline" — sounds like a scolding parent.
  Say "control" and "clarity."
- Don't over-explain features — one live demo of "log an M-Pesa expense
  -> see your leak" beats ten slides.
- Don't ignore trust — money app = "will you steal my data / my money?"
  Address it head-on: free, your data stays yours, we don't sell it
  (the privacy policy backs this up — use it as a trust point).


A 20-SECOND VERSION YOU CAN ACTUALLY SAY
----------------------------------------
"Most young Kenyans are hustling hard but broke by mid-month — money
 comes, money vanishes, and one emergency sends you straight to Fuliza.
 PesaFlow is the first money app built for how we actually earn — daily,
 from side hustles — that shows you exactly where your money goes, helps
 you save small, and turns 'broke till end month' into building a plot or
 a business. Free, in your language, your data stays yours."
