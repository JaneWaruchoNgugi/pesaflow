----------------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT IS PESAFLOW?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

PesaFlow is a personal finance app built for the Kenyan reality —
M-Pesa, SACCOs, money market funds, daily incomes and real budgets.

In one place it lets you track your spending, plan your savings goals,
watch your investments, calculate your true net worth, and get an AI
advisor that answers your money questions any time. It also comes with
a free Financial Learning Hub (blog) so you can actually learn how to
handle money — not just track it.

The goal is simple: help everyday Kenyans understand where their money
goes, make smarter decisions, and grow their wealth over time.
